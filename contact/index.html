Write to the studio

The studio is one person, and every message is read.

Asking to be told when the game is on Steam means one email, on the day the Steam page opens, and nothing after it. Press and creators: write from the address your channel or publication shows (press kit). If the game has gone wrong, the support page says what to send.

The form is delivered by FormSubmit, which may ask you to tick a box to show you are not a robot. What you send is used to answer you and nothing else. You can also write directly to hello@primemoverindustries.com.
